I am delighted to share that I have been awarded a UZH Postdoc Grant from the University of Zurich.

The grant begins in December 2026 and will fund a project with [Katharina Wilmes](https://www.ini.uzh.ch/en/research/groups/wilmes.html) at the Institute of Neuroinformatics (INI), exploring the different neural mechanisms underlying uncertainty weighting and attentional modulation in predictive coding networks.
