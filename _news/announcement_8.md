I co-led the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/telluride-2026/topic-areas/sync26-spatiotemporal-dynamics-in-neural-computation">Spatiotemporal dYnamics in Neural Computation (SYNC)</a> topic area at the 2026 Telluride Neuromorphic AI Workshop. We had 8 talks and tutorials on topics ranging from Bayesian brain models to cortical traveling waves, and a great group of participants who did projects on fast weights, multimodal predictive coding, action decoding from EEG, detection of traveling waves in EEG, and cognitive mapping with adaptive spatial resolution.
